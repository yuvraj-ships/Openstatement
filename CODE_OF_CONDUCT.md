# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be patient with newcomers (many contributors are adding their first open-source change by sharing their bank's layout), assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.

Harassment, personal attacks and sharing other people's private data, including bank details, are not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the email on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
